# distill-candidate-profile-for-opportunity-matching

End-to-end flow that inventories sources, captures and grounds preferences/salary, produces proof variants, and injects constraints into CV analysis/prep for accurate opportunity packets

## Skill chain

1. `capture-preferences-to-artifact`
2. `ground-salary-benchmarks`
3. `draft-proof-variants-from-cv`
4. `prefer-constraints-in-analyze-prep`

## Phases

### Inventory

Read plan, distillation sources, cvdata and fit schemas

### Capture

Write user answers on work mode, geo, cash floor into candidate-preferences.md

### Ground

Fetch salary benchmarks and update FT/contract ranges

### Generate

Draft proof variants and monthly savings from CV

### Integrate

Explain and plan wiring of constraints into analyze/prep
