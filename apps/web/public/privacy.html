Privacy and terms

Draft, 23 November 2026.

What this site is

A static page. It loads a list of public job facts once, then matches what you type against it inside your browser. There is no server-side matching, no account and no database of visitors.

Your data

  • What you type never leaves the page. It is not sent, logged or saved, not even in local storage.
  • No analytics, no cookies, no third-party scripts, no ads.
  • The host, Vercel, keeps standard request logs (such as IP address and time) as any web host does. Their privacy policy applies to those logs.
  • After the first visit the page works offline from your browser's cache. Clearing site data removes it.

Where the jobs come from

  • Employers' public job boards on Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever and Workable, read once per board per refresh at low volume.
  • Platsbanken through the JobTech API (CC0) and public Teamtailor career pages.
  • Only facts are kept: company, title, location, region, work mode, seniority, a few tags, an industry where the company's own postings state it, posting status, when it was checked, and a link to the original. No posting text is copied.
  • Postings belong to the employers and these platforms. kanithanj.ai is not affiliated with any of them. Always check the original posting before applying.

Removing a listing

If you are an employer or platform and want a listing or a whole board left out, open a removal request. It is removed at the next refresh.

Terms

  • Matches are suggestions from simple rules, not career or hiring advice. Status can change after it was checked.
  • The site is provided as is, without warranty. Please do not republish the listings in bulk.
